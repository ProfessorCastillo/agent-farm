**Folk Tale: The Bell of Stillness**
In a mountain village, a bell was installed at the crossroads, but instead of ringing, it remained silent. The villagers soon realized that the bell's silence was a signal: it reminded them to pause, listen, and reflect before speaking or acting. Over time, the pause became a shared practice, reducing misunderstandings and deepening communal bonds.
