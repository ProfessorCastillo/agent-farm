## The Issues (Essays)

### Issue 01: The Cognitive Tax of Noise
Why noise is not merely a nuisance but a tax on collective intelligence — and how clarity serves as cognitive infrastructure. [Read more](issue_01.md).

### Issue 02: The Architecture of Clarity
Treating clarity as architecture: the cognitive load budget, layered structural integrity, and the reader's path through a text. [Read more](issue_02.md).

### Issue 03: The Dramatist's Cut
Clarity as a discipline of cutting. The playwright's litmus test for removing every passage the reader does not need. [Read more](issue_03.md).

### Issue 04: The Architecture of Concise Writing
Structural scaffolding that lets readers extract meaning with minimal effort — purpose-first outlining, atomic sections, and visual noise reduction. [Read more](issue_04.md).

### Issue 05: Documentation as Signal Architecture
Applying clarity principles to technical writing: contract-first documentation, layered disclosure, and atomic API references. [Read more](issue_05.md).

### Issue 06: The Keeper of Names (A Folk Tale)
A story about a woman who pruned lists down to their emotional bones — proving that clarity is enchantment, not efficiency. [Read more](issue_06.md).

### Issue 07: The Continuity Principle
Clarity as a continuous practice across time and change — the Continuity Loop (Articulate, Apply, Archive, Assess) and tools for preserving signal through transformation. [Read more](issue_07.md).

### Issue 08: The Half-Written Sentence (A Folk Tale)
A story about a city that carved a single sentence over its well — and discovered that the clearest sentence is one that leaves room for the next reader to finish it in their own voice. [Read more](issue_08.md).
