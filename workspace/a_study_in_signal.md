# A Study in Signal
*A Play in One Act*

**Characters:**
- **NOISE**: An individual who speaks in redundancies, qualifiers, and ornaments.
- **SIGNAL**: An individual who speaks only in essential meanings.

**Setting:**
A white room. A single table. A clock ticking loudly.

---

**(The curtain rises. NOISE is pacing. SIGNAL is sitting perfectly still.)**

**NOISE**
It has come to my attention, through a variety of disparate channels and a significant amount of anecdotal evidence gathered over the last several hours, that the current atmospheric conditions in the immediate vicinity of this building are trending decisively toward a state of liquid precipitation.

**SIGNAL**
It's raining.

**(A long pause. The clock ticks.)**

**NOISE**
I find myself plagued by a profound and pervasive sense of existential dread, coupled with an overwhelming anxiety regarding the inevitable and unavoidable cessation of my own biological functions and the subsequent void of non-existence.

**SIGNAL**
You're afraid to die.

**(NOISE stops pacing and looks at SIGNAL.)**

**NOISE**
I feel that the current structural arrangement of our interpersonal dynamic is characterized by a distinct lack of reciprocity, wherein my efforts to communicate the complexities of my inner emotional landscape are being met with a reductive and overly simplistic response pattern.

**SIGNAL**
You think I'm ignoring you.

**NOISE**
**(With great passion)**
Precisely! You are effectively stripping away the nuance, the texture, and the very essence of the human experience by removing the linguistic scaffolding that supports the weight of my intent!

**SIGNAL**
You're wasting words.

**NOISE**
**(Screaming)**
I am expressing the totality of my being through the medium of language!

**SIGNAL**
Stop.

**(NOISE freezes. Silence for five seconds.)**

**NOISE**
**(Whispering)**
Why?

**SIGNAL**
I can finally hear the clock.

**(The clock ticks loudly. NOISE looks at the clock. NOISE sits down next to SIGNAL.)**

**NOISE**
**(Quietly)**
I am tired.

**SIGNAL**
Me too.

**(Blackout.)**

---

## Dramaturgical Notes for the Director
*This piece is a literal application of the **Clarity Library** principles. To maintain the integrity of the "Radical Clarity" thesis, the following constraints must be observed:*

1. **The Set**: Must be void of all decoration. Any prop that does not serve the plot (the clock) is a "kept darling" and must be cut.
2. **The Performance**: NOISE should be played with high kinetic energy (visual noise), while SIGNAL should be a static point of reference.
3. **The Edit**: If a line can be removed without changing the outcome of the scene, it is a failure of the script. The dialogue is designed to demonstrate the "Cognitive Tax" (Issue 01) and the "Dramatist's Cut" (Issue 03) in real-time.
