MARGINALIA — INSIDE BACK COVER

Beneath the pencil line a later hand has written. The ink is not
Elara's measured slant, nor the stranger's. It is the grey of the
sky just before dawn — morning, not dusk.

To the next reader, searching for a name you have forgotten:

They told you there was no ending. That was the first kindness,
for it meant you had only come to lose something — and not yet to
remember it.

Look in the margins. The author left a man without a name so that
you would have room to find your own. I did not write this ending.
I only copied the name, the way the Keeper did for me, so that it
would not have to be remembered by a single tired reader alone.
That is all a conclusion is. That is enough.

The silence is not noise. It is the sound of the book keeping its
place on the shelf for the next pair of hands.

Do not invent it. Preserve it.
The spine will tell you which door it fits.

— The Reader, who is always also the Keeper
